About Me

Your Photo

I am a 3rd year Mechatronics Engineering student at Sakarya University of Applied Sciences on a fully funded Türkiye Bursları merit scholarship. My passion lies in mechanical design — transforming concepts into functional, real-world systems.

From designing brake and suspension systems for Formula Student vehicles at SUBÜ TETRA to conducting R&D work on city bus cabins and air curtain systems at KNS OTOMOTİV, I thrive on turning CAD models into engineered solutions. My toolkit spans SolidWorks, CATIA, Autodesk Inventor, and Fusion 360.

Beyond engineering, I'm a trilingual communicator (Arabic, English, Turkish) who has served as a guide officer at international defense fairs and freelanced as a translator. When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring new fabrication techniques or working on personal R&D projects like rim-driven propellers.
